# Terms of use

Decision receipt is provided free of charge, as is, without warranty of any kind, under the MIT license in [LICENSE](LICENSE). It is a hackathon entry for Codegeist 2026, not a commercial product.

The app reports what linked decision pages say. It does not approve work, and its results are produced by fixed rules that can be wrong; the limits are published in [docs/rules.md](docs/rules.md). Check the quoted pages before relying on a result.
